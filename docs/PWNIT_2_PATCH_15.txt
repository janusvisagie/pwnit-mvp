PwnIt 2 — Patch 15: Welcome-modal copy to current model
=======================================================
Changes the welcome modal's free-allowance line from
  "You receive 30 free credits per day."
to
  "You get 6 free plays a day — and every paid play (R5) builds your discount."

Why: 30 free credits at R5/play = 6 free plays/day; the new wording is game-first
and on-model (play / discount / win-or-buy). Accurate to DAILY_FREE_CREDITS=30 and
PWNIT2_PLAY_COST_CREDITS=5.

The credits page (/buy-credits) already uses generic "free daily credits" copy, so
it needs no change. (A legacy PwnIt-1 page, /item/[id], still mentions "30 daily
free credits" — that is the old registration flow, not part of PwnIt 2; left as-is.)
