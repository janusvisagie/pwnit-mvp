PwnIt 2 — Patch 18: Free-play model + practice toggle + podium bonus discount
=============================================================================

1) FREE-PLAY MODEL
   • One free COMPETITIVE play per day (counts toward the leaderboard, earns no
     discount — your free entry route). Implemented by setting the daily free grant
     to exactly one play: DAILY_FREE_CREDITS 30 -> 5 (1 credit = R1, play = R5).
   • Unlimited free PRACTICE: a toggle on the game screen. Practice runs the gauntlet
     entirely client-side (local seed, no token, no score POST) — no credit spent, no
     leaderboard entry, no discount. Reinstates the old practice-toggle idea.
   • Paid plays (R5) still count, build R1:R1 discount, and fund activation.

   Note: existing users keep whatever free balance they have today; the new 5/day
   grant applies from their next daily top-up. The "Buy 30 credits" top-up bundle is
   a PURCHASE pack (unrelated to the free grant) and is unchanged.

2) PODIUM BONUS (percentage of paid spend)
   • Rank 1 still WINS the voucher.
   • Once standings are final (status window), rank 2 gets +10% and rank 3 gets +5%
     of THEIR OWN paid spend as extra discount, stacked on the R1:R1 they earned.
   • Applied at purchase only (it reduces what a runner-up pays to buy the voucher);
     surfaced as its own line in the purchase breakdown, and recorded in the ledger.
   • Easy to tweak/remove: edit the two percentages in buildPurchaseQuote
     (pwnit2CampaignServer.ts). Set both to 0 to disable.

3) "HOW IT WORKS" + MODAL COPY updated to describe free play, practice, and the
   runner-up bonus.
