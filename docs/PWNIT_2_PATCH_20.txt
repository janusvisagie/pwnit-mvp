PwnIt 2 — Patch 20: Two more gauntlet disciplines (Stroop + "What comes next")
=============================================================================

The PwnIt Gauntlet now cycles through FIVE skill disciplines (% 5):
  0 memory   watch a growing colour sequence, tap it back
  1 search   Schulte grid: tap the numbers 1..K in order
  2 math      tap the correct answer to a chain of problems
  3 stroop    tap the INK COLOUR of a colour-word (not the word) — attention/inhibition
  4 pattern   "what comes next": tap the next term in a short logical sequence

math/stroop/pattern share one interaction (a chain of multiple-choice items), so the
client renders them through a single "choice" path. Difficulty still rises once per full
cycle (every 5 rounds). Everything stays server-validated: the server re-derives each
round — including the Stroop ink colour and the pattern's next term — from the signed
seed and never trusts a client score.

API: unchanged. Score body is still { token, slug, rounds: number[][], … }; for math,
stroop and pattern, rounds[i] is the array of chosen OPTION INDICES.

NO DB migration. No env / route / server changes.
