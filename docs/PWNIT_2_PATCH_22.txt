PwnIt 2 — Patch 22: "1 free play left" + discard old 30-credit balances + credits-page cleanup
==============================================================================================

This finishes the free-play model on the surface:

1) "1 free play left" instead of "5 credits" (#1)
   The credits pill now reads the free balance as PLAYS: it shows "Today: N free play(s)"
   (free credits / R5), with paid credit and "practice is always free" underneath —
   instead of a raw credit count.

2) Discard previous 30-credit balances (#1)
   Since we are still testing, the daily top-up now clamps any free balance above the
   daily grant (DAILY_FREE_CREDITS = 5) down to the grant — on the very next request, in
   both the demo and real top-up paths. So leftover 30-credit balances become "1 free
   play" immediately instead of lingering until the next day. (Only FREE credits are
   touched; purchased/paid credits are untouched.)

3) Credits page cleanup (#1)
   /buy-credits no longer references "Number Chain Sprint", "Checkers", or "30 credits".
   It now states the real model: 1 free play/day, free unlimited practice, R5 extra plays
   that build R1:R1 discount.
